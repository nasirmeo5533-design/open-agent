For years, the question was whether ChatGPT would ever carry advertising. In 2026 it does. OpenAI began testing ads in ChatGPT early in the year and has since opened buying to more advertisers. For businesses, **ChatGPT ads** are a new way to appear at the moment someone is asking an AI assistant for help with a decision.

This guide explains how ChatGPT ads work based on OpenAI's own announcements, who sees them, how buying works, what advertisers can and cannot access, and which businesses should test them now.

**Short answer:** ChatGPT ads appear at the bottom of answers when there is a relevant sponsored product or service, are clearly labelled, and are shown to users on the Free and Go plans. Advertisers can buy through agency and technology partners or through a beta self-serve Ads Manager, on CPM or CPC. Advertisers receive aggregated performance data but not users' conversations.

## How ChatGPT ads work

In its January 2026 post on its approach to advertising, OpenAI said it planned to test ads "at the bottom of answers in ChatGPT when there's a relevant sponsored product or service based on your current conversation" ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/our-approach-to-advertising-and-expanding-access/)). OpenAI also stated that ads are clearly labelled and separated from the answer itself, and that users can see why they were shown an ad or dismiss it.

The key difference from search ads is context. Instead of matching a short keyword typed into a search box, the ad is matched to what the person is discussing in the conversation.

### Who sees ChatGPT ads

According to OpenAI, ads are shown on the Free and Go tiers. OpenAI stated that Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise subscriptions do not include ads ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/our-approach-to-advertising-and-expanding-access/)).

### Where ads are not shown

OpenAI stated that ads are not shown to users it knows or predicts are under 18, and that ads are not eligible to appear near sensitive or regulated topics such as health, mental health or politics.

## How to buy ChatGPT ads

In May 2026, OpenAI announced new ways to buy ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/new-ways-to-buy-chatgpt-ads/)):

- **Through partners:** large agency groups (Dentsu, Omnicom, Publicis and WPP) and technology partners including Adobe, Criteo, Kargo, Pacvue and StackAdapt.
- **Through a self-serve Ads Manager** in beta, opening gradually, where advertisers register, set budgets, bids and pacing, upload ads and view results.
- **Pricing:** the early pilot ran on CPM (cost per thousand impressions). OpenAI added CPC bidding, where advertisers are charged based on clicks, and said both models continue.

Availability has been expanding by country through 2026, and reports from trade press differ on exact markets and dates. Check [OpenAI's advertising site](https://ads.openai.com/) for current eligibility in your country before planning a campaign.

## What advertisers can and can't see

This is the question most businesses ask first. OpenAI's stated position:

- Conversations are kept private from advertisers, and OpenAI says it does not sell user data to advertisers.
- Advertisers receive **aggregated performance insights**, not individual conversations.
- Measurement uses familiar tools: a pixel on your site and a conversions API, similar to how [the Meta Conversions API](meta-conversions-api.html) works.

So you can measure clicks and conversions, but you won't get conversation transcripts or personal details.

## ChatGPT ads vs Google Ads

| | ChatGPT ads | Google Search ads |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger | Context of a conversation | Keywords in a search |
| Placement | Below the AI answer | Above and below search results |
| Buying | Partners or beta Ads Manager; CPM or CPC | Self-serve, mature auction, many bid strategies |
| Data and tools | New, still developing | Very mature reporting and targeting |
| Maturity | Early; formats and rules still changing | Established for over two decades |

ChatGPT ads are not a replacement for [Google Ads](google-ads-budget-small-business.html). For most businesses they are a test budget alongside proven channels.

## Which businesses should test ChatGPT ads now

ChatGPT ads are most promising where people use AI assistants to research decisions:

- **Software and SaaS:** people ask ChatGPT to compare tools and recommend options.
- **Considered consumer purchases:** electronics, travel, home improvement, education.
- **Ecommerce brands** with clear products and good landing pages.
- **B2B services** where buyers research providers before contacting them.

They are less suitable for categories OpenAI restricts (health, politics and other sensitive topics), very local businesses in markets where self-serve access isn't available yet, and businesses without conversion tracking.

## How to run a sensible first test

1. **Confirm eligibility** in your market and category on OpenAI's advertising site.
2. **Set up tracking first:** pixel and conversions API on the pages ads will send people to.
3. **Write ads that help,** not shout. The ad sits under a helpful answer; a clear, specific offer fits better than hype.
4. **Use a focused landing page** that continues the conversation the person was having.
5. **Start with a fixed test budget** and a clear success measure (cost per lead or sale).
6. **Compare with existing channels** after a few weeks, on the same conversion measure.

## Ads are only half of AI visibility

Paid placement is one way to show up in ChatGPT. The other is being mentioned in the answers themselves. That depends on how clearly your website explains what you do, whether other trusted sites mention you, and whether AI systems can crawl your pages. Optimising for AI answers is often called generative engine optimisation (GEO), and it works alongside paid ads.

## Conclusion

ChatGPT ads are new, real and still changing. They appear below relevant answers, reach Free and Go users, and can be bought through partners or a beta Ads Manager on CPM or CPC. For businesses whose customers research decisions with AI, a measured test makes sense now, as long as tracking is in place and expectations are realistic.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does ChatGPT have ads now?

Yes. OpenAI began testing ads in ChatGPT in 2026 and has expanded buying options since, including a beta self-serve Ads Manager and CPC bidding announced in May 2026. Ads appear below relevant answers for users on the Free and Go plans.

### Do paid ChatGPT plans show ads?

According to OpenAI, ads are shown on the Free and Go tiers, while Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise subscriptions do not include ads. This may change in future, so check OpenAI's current policy.

### How much do ChatGPT ads cost?

OpenAI sells ads on a CPM basis (per thousand impressions) and has added CPC bidding (per click). Actual costs depend on competition, targeting and market. Trade press has reported early pricing ranges, but they vary by source, so start with a fixed test budget and measure cost per conversion.

### Can advertisers see my ChatGPT conversations?

OpenAI says no. It states that conversations are kept private from advertisers and that advertisers receive only aggregated performance insights. Advertisers measure results with their own pixel and conversion data, not with access to conversations.

### How do I start advertising on ChatGPT?

Check OpenAI's advertising site for availability in your market, then either apply for the beta self-serve Ads Manager or work with one of OpenAI's agency or technology partners. Set up conversion tracking on your landing pages before launching.

### Are ChatGPT ads better than Google Ads?

They are different. Google Ads is mature, with detailed targeting, reporting and years of benchmarks. ChatGPT ads are new and reach people during AI conversations. For most businesses, ChatGPT ads are a test alongside Google Ads, not a replacement.
