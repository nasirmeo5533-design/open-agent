About Me

Hi, I'm Abeer Nasir.

Generative AI specialist, AI agent developer, and digital marketing specialist. Based in Karachi, Pakistan helping small businesses in Pakistan and the US.

Who I Am

I started learning Generative AI in 2023 with one simple question: how can AI help a business work better? I didn't come from a traditional tech background, and I didn't follow a fixed roadmap. I learned by building projects, working with real clients, and solving real problems.

Today I help businesses use AI and digital marketing to improve their workflows, strengthen their online presence, and make better use of technology. I enjoy turning ideas into practical solutions that save time, cut repetitive work, and support growth.

I've worked with e-commerce and D2C brands especially in the perfume and beauty industry helping them set up Shopify stores, run Meta Ads, create AI-assisted marketing content, and automate everyday business processes with no-code AI tools.

Outside client work, I share what I learn. I've written free ebooks on Generative AI and digital marketing to help beginners, business owners, and marketers understand these topics in a practical way.
