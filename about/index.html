Who We Are

OpenAgent was built around a simple idea: high-value project businesses deserve a growth system, not scattered tactics. We combine marketing strategy, paid ads, AI automation and website development into one coherent engine that generates and manages qualified enquiries.

We work with businesses where every new customer is a significant project — fit-out contractors, healthcare clinics, luxury interior designers, hospitality groups, and commercial landscapers. These businesses don't need more clicks. They need fewer, better enquiries that turn into real projects.

Our approach: launch targeted ad campaigns, capture enquiries through conversion-optimised landing pages, qualify leads with AI, sync everything to your CRM, and hand qualified opportunities to your sales team. Marketing, AI and automation working together.
