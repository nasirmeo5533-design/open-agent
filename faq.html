FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before working with us. Can't find your answer? Just ask.

What services do you offer?

Meta Ads, Shopify development, AI automation, AI agents, AI content, web development, Google & Meta paid ads, and social media marketing.

Do you work with US-based businesses?

Yes. I work remotely with US e-commerce and D2C founders. Communication is over WhatsApp, email or video call, and I align deliverables to US time zones where needed.

Where are you based?

Karachi, Pakistan. I work with clients worldwide remotely.

How do we communicate?

Fastest is WhatsApp (+92 370 3159642). Otherwise email or the contact form. I reply within a few hours.

Can you help my perfume / beauty brand?

Yes — I've worked with e-commerce and D2C brands in perfume and beauty on Meta Ads, Shopify and content.

What exactly do you do?

I help brands grow with Meta Ads, Shopify, AI automation, AI agents, AI content and web development. Think of me as your AI-powered marketing partner.

Do you build chatbots / AI agents?

Yes — custom chatbots and virtual assistants for support and lead generation, built on LLMs like ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini.

How long until we see results?

Meta Ads campaigns typically show early signal in 1–2 weeks; meaningful ROAS data in 3–4 weeks. Automation and content work land faster — often within the first week.

Do you do long contracts?

No lock-in. We start with a clear project scope, then optimize month by month. You can pause anytime.

What's your pricing?

Transparent monthly plans: Starter PKR 30,000, Premium PKR 50,000, Pro PKR 80,000. No hidden fees, no "contact for price" — see the Pricing page.

What is the money-back guarantee?

Every plan has a 4-day money-back guarantee: if you're not satisfied within the first 4 days, you get a full refund of the service fee, no questions asked. Full details on the Refund Policy page.

Do I need a big budget?

No. I've run small-budget Meta Ads tests for D2C brands and lead-generation tests for local services — and small budgets force discipline, which converts better. Plans start at PKR 30,000/mo.

What AI tools do you use?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini for AI content and agents. Zapier, Make, n8n for automation. Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads for paid campaigns. Shopify for e-commerce.

Do you write code?

I build no-code automations and workflows, plus simple web pages like this one. For complex custom development, I partner with trusted developers.

Can I see my ad account?

You own your ad account and pixels from day one. Full transparency — you can log in anytime.
