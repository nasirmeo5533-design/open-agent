About Me

Hi, I'm Abeer Nasir the person behind Open Agent.

I started learning about AI in 2023 with one simple question: how can AI help a real business work better? So I learned by building projects and working with real clients solving real problems, not reading theory.

Today I help stores and small businesses get more customers, save time, and grow. I keep everything simple: you tell me the problem, I fix it, and I explain it in plain English.
