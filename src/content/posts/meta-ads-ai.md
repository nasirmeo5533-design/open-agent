## The real problem

Most campaigns fail for three reasons.

First, the audience is wrong. You target people who look like buyers, but they never buy.

Second, the creative is tired. The same image runs for weeks. People scroll past.

Third, the data is late. You check results once a week. By then, the budget is gone.

## How AI changes the game

AI reads the numbers live. It spots a winning creative in hours, not days.

It shifts budget toward the audience that already converts. It pauses the losers. It writes new ad angles in seconds.

The result is simple: more sales for the same budget.
