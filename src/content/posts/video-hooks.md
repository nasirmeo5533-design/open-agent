People scroll fast.

Your video gets five seconds. Maybe fewer.

If the first five seconds do not hook them, they are gone. The best product in the world will not save you.

## The hook comes first

Never start with your logo. Never start slow.

Start with the payoff. Show the result. Make a bold claim. Start mid-action.

The viewer should ask "wait, what?" That is the hook.

## Keep the energy up

Editing is not just cutting clips. It is pacing.

Change the shot every few seconds. Add motion. Add text that lands with the beat.

Boredom is the enemy. Every frame should earn the next one.

## The last three seconds

End with a clear ask. One action. No choices.

"Get it before it sells out." That is enough.

## What works for ads

Short beats long. One idea beats five ideas.

UGC-style videos feel real, so they convert. Polished ads build trust, so they work for brand.

We edit both. The right one depends on your goal.

## The bottom line

Great video does not waste a second.

Hooks in five. Proof in the middle. One ask at the end.

That is how you stop the scroll. That is how you win the sale.
