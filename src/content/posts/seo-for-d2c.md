People search before they buy.

They type "best perfume for men" or "wireless earbuds under $50". Then they click the top result.

If that result is not your store, you just lost a sale.

## Why most stores never rank

Most stores build SEO once and forget it.

Their product pages say the same thing as a hundred other stores. Google sees no reason to rank them.

They also ignore the boring stuff. Slow pages. Broken links. No clear titles.

SEO is not magic. It is doing the basics better than your competitors.

## The checklist that works

Start with these four things.

**Fix the technical side.** Make your site fast. Make it easy for Google to read.

**Write for your buyer.** Use the words they type into Google. Put them in titles, headings, and product descriptions.

**Build real content.** Answer real questions. A blog post that helps a shopper beats a product page nobody reads.

**Track everything.** Know which pages rank and which ones stall.

## E-commerce SEO is a system

It is not one fix. It is a loop.

Find keywords. Create content. Measure. Improve. Repeat.

We run this loop every month. Stores climb the rankings. Sales follow.

## The bottom line

Your competitors are not better than you. They are just easier to find.

Fix that, and the traffic comes to you. For free.
