Overview

Give Every Customer An Instant, Intelligent Response

An AI agent is more than a chatbot — it is a digital team member that reads, decides and acts on its own. We design, build and connect agents that handle the repetitive work eating your team’s hours: replying to emails, answering sales questions, qualifying leads and moving data between your tools.

Because we build on the current agent ecosystem — OpenAI, Hermes Agent, Open Manus Bot, Paperclip and more — we pick the right brain for each job and keep your costs predictable.

  • Customer-facing agents that answer instantly on your site, WhatsApp or email
  • Email auto-reply agents that draft, send and follow up for you
  • Lead triage that qualifies and routes enquiries to the right person
  • Integration with your CRM, inbox, calendar and databases
  • Built on OpenAI, Hermes Agent, Open Manus Bot, Paperclip & more

Agent Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

An AI agent is a program that completes tasks autonomously — chatting with customers, answering emails, doing research or moving data between apps — based on rules and goals we define for your business, using a large language model as its brain.

We work across the modern agent stack — OpenAI assistants, Hermes Agent, Open Manus Bot, Paperclip and other open-source agent frameworks — and pick the best fit for your use case and budget rather than forcing one vendor.

A chatbot only replies in a chat window. An agent actually does things: it can read and send email, update your CRM, book appointments, create tickets and trigger workflows — closing the loop instead of just talking.

A first working version of a typical agent ships in one to two weeks. More complex multi-step agents take longer depending on the systems they need to connect to. We always show you a live prototype before we scale it.
