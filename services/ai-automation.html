Overview

Stop Doing The Same Task Twice

Every business runs on a handful of repeatable processes — responding to enquiries, updating records, chasing follow-ups, preparing reports. Our AI automation removes the manual effort behind those processes and makes them run reliably, every time.

We connect the tools you already use — email, WhatsApp, sheets, CRMs and databases — and add AI that can read, judge and respond. The result is a smoother operation that never forgets a lead and never drops a ball.

  • Instant lead follow-up across email and WhatsApp
  • Automatic data entry and syncing between your tools
  • AI-powered inbox and task triage
  • Scheduled reports generated and sent for you
  • Workflows built on your existing software stack
