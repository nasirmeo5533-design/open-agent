Why most small businesses lose money on ads

Running ads isn't the hard part. Running them well is. Most small businesses in Karachi lose money on ads for the same simple reasons: no plan, no testing, and stopping too early. They put money on Facebook and Instagram without knowing who to target or what message will work. The platform spends their budget on the wrong people, nothing happens, and they give up — then say ads don't work. The problem isn't ads. It's how they're set up.

The same story repeats for small businesses in the United States, just with a bigger budget and more competition. A small shop in the US can burn through hundreds of dollars in a week if the targeting and the offer are weak. The platforms are happy to take the money either way. Someone has to make sure every rupee and dollar is pointed at people who might actually buy.

This is where a plan comes in. Before we spend anything, we decide who you're selling to, what you're offering, and how we'll measure success. Then we test small, find what works, and put more budget behind it.

What you get

You don't get a login and a pat on the back. You get a person running your campaigns properly. Here's what that looks like:

  • Campaign setup on Facebook, Instagram, and Google — the platforms your customers actually use
  • Audience targeting based on location, interests, and behavior — no guessing
  • Ad copy and creative written for your business, not a template
  • Daily checks and small tweaks to keep costs down and results up
  • A/B testing, so we know which headline, image, and audience really work
  • Clear reports: what was spent, what sold, and what we'll do next
  • Landing page advice, because a good ad sent to a weak page still wastes money

How it works

  • Free first call — we talk about your business, your budget, and your goal.
  • Strategy and setup, days 1 to 7 — I build your campaigns, audiences, and ads, and you approve everything before launch.
  • Launch and test, week 2 — campaigns go live and we start learning what works.
  • Daily optimization and weekly reports — I adjust what's underperforming and send you a plain-English update.

Ad spend is separate — here's exactly what that means

My fee covers the management: strategy, setup, ad creative, testing, and daily optimization. The money that actually goes to Facebook and Google is separate. You pay that directly to the platforms, and you approve it before it's spent. There are no hidden charges and no surprise bills. You always know where your money is going.

This is standard practice, and it's the honest way to run ads. I never mark up your ad spend, and I never get a cut of what you pay the platforms. My only income is the management fee — so my incentive is simple: make your campaigns work.

How fast will you see results?

Here's an honest answer: no one can promise sales in a week. What I can promise is fast, honest feedback. In the first 1 to 2 weeks you'll see real data — how many people saw your ads, clicked, and took action. That tells us what's working and what needs fixing.

For most small businesses in Pakistan and the US, meaningful results show up in 2 to 4 weeks: more messages, more calls, more orders. Campaigns that perform well get more budget. Campaigns that don't get changed or paused. The faster we learn, the faster you win.
