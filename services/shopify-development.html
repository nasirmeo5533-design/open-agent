Getting Visits but Not Enough Sales?

Small problems in a store add up to abandoned carts.

Many Shopify stores run on a theme that was never fully set up. Product pages load slowly, collections are hard to browse, apps conflict with each other and nobody is sure the tracking is right. I fix the store's foundations first, then improve the pages that lead to a sale.

Book a Free Strategy Call

Common Issues I Fix:

  • Slow product and collection pages
  • Too many apps slowing the store down
  • A theme that breaks when you change it
  • Shipping and checkout settings that confuse buyers
  • No reliable tracking from add-to-cart to purchase

My Promise:

Every change is judged by one test: does it make it easier for a shopper to find a product and buy it?

From Store Setup to a Store That Sells

Whether you're launching a new store, moving from another platform or fixing one that already runs, the work is scoped around your products and the way your customers shop.

What's Included:
