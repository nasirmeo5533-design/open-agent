Videos Edited To Hold Attention.

Property walk-throughs, project reveals and ad creatives edited for pacing, clarity and the platform they live on.

Overview

Sharp, Platform-Ready Edits

People scroll fast, so every second of a video has to earn its place. I edit short-form content and advertising creative with strong hooks at the start, tight pacing through the middle and a clear message at the end.

Whether you are feeding organic content or paid ads, the goal is the same: a video that communicates your point quickly and keeps viewers engaged long enough to act.

  • Short-form social videos
  • Advertising creatives
  • Captions, graphics & motion
  • Sound design & music
  • Vertical & horizontal formats

What Editing Involves

Each edit is cut around a clear goal. These are the elements I focus on:

  • Hook-first cutting
  • Captions & on-screen text
  • Sound design & pacing
  • Platform-ready formats
