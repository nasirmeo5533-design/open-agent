Performance Marketing

Every campaign carries a defined job.

I plan, launch and manage paid campaigns on Google and Meta for businesses in Pakistan and the U.S. Each one earns its budget against a stated objective: leads, sales or booked calls, with tracking that can actually verify it.

The approach

Context before spend.

Paid performance rests on a handful of things: the offer, the creative, the audience, the budget and what the tracking can measure. I start with those five, fix what can be fixed, and only then talk about scale.

One clear objective per campaign
Structure agreed before creative goes live
Weekly optimization with written notes
Honest reporting without fabricated guarantees
What is included

Paid media, scoped to your business.

The written scope comes first: deliverables, cadence, budget and the shape of the reporting.

Google Ads

Search, display, YouTube and remarketing campaigns built around the queries and intent that actually sell what you sell.

Meta Ads

Facebook and Instagram campaigns with structured audiences, placements matched to the objective and creative that gets tested properly.

Campaign strategy

A funnel agreed upfront: who sees the ad, what they see next, and which action counts as a win.

Creative and copy

Hooks, angles and variations written per platform, so the tests produce lessons you can reuse.

Tracking and reporting

Conversion events set up correctly and summarized weekly in plain language: what happened, and what changes next.

Budget management

Spend reviewed continuously and moved toward what performs. Waste gets cut early, not at the end of the month.

Google Ads

Meet demand while it happens.

Search campaigns reach people who are already looking for a solution. The work is keyword structure, ad copy that matches the query and bids that respect the margin in your business. Reports cover spend, conversions, click-through and cost per result, with the next round of changes written down.

Search, display, YouTube and remarketing structures
Keyword research mapped to your actual offers
Ad copy tested against the same objective
Bid and budget review on a fixed weekly cycle
Facebook & Instagram

Build demand before the search.

Meta works when creative and audience carry the weight. Campaigns are structured by objective, audiences built from your real buyer profile, and creative tests scored honestly so they end arguments instead of starting them. Retargeting runs in the background: people who watched, visited or added to cart get a next step.

Campaigns modeled on reach, traffic or conversion objectives
Audience building from interests, custom lists and retargeting pools
Creative variants with a scoring rule everyone can see
Frequency and fatigue checks built into the routine
Optimization

A system that keeps learning.

Most paid accounts lose money in dull ways: split focus, missing tracking, budgets parked in campaigns that stopped working weeks ago. Optimization here is a fixed weekly routine: read the data, make changes, write them down. When more than one platform is in play, budgets follow results instead of habit.

Weekly optimization with a written change log
Multi-channel planning where two or more platforms run
Landing page and message alignment reviewed regularly
Clear reporting on leads, sales and cost per result
Markets

Built for your market, not a template.

Pakistan and the U.S. are different paid media environments. Costs, payment flows, language context and buying behavior all differ. Campaign copy, landing pages and budget plans are made for the market they will run in, instead of one setup stretched across both.

Pakistan: local platforms, payment realities and language context
U.S.: competitive search and social environments
Budget plans in the currency you work in
Process

How the work runs.

01

Audit

Existing accounts, current results, funnel and tracking get reviewed. When nothing exists, this becomes a shorter call instead.

02

Plan

Objective, audiences, budgets, creative direction and the measurement plan, all in writing before launch.

03

Launch

Campaigns built and published, tracking verified, and the first experiments put on a calendar.

04

Improve

Weekly optimization, written notes and reports that say what changed and why.
