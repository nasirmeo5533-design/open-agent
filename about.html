Our Story

Built On Accountability & Commercial Precision

OpenAgent was founded on a straightforward principle: businesses need marketing partners who operate with financial discipline, transparent reporting, and deep strategic accountability.

Over the past five years, we have grown from a small consultancy into a full-service agency handling search optimization, paid ads across multiple channels, social media, and business websites. Our partners range from high-growth Pakistani retailers to international B2B companies across the GCC, UK, and North America.

  • Dedicated account leadership with direct founder oversight
  • Strategies built around what it actually costs you to win each customer
  • Live reporting dashboards with verified lead and revenue attribution
  • Multi-disciplinary in-house team across all five core digital disciplines
