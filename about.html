About OpenAgent

Digital Marketing for Real Estate & Interior Design.

OpenAgent is my digital marketing studio, focused on two industries I know inside out. I combine Meta & Google Ads, SEO, websites, content and AI automation to help property and design businesses attract the high-value clients they deserve.

My Approach

I Start With The Problem, Not The Technology.

Every business has different needs. Instead of recommending technology simply because it is new or popular, I first try to understand what the business is trying to achieve.

From there, I identify where AI, automation, development or digital marketing can provide a practical solution.

The goal is simple:

  • Build what is useful.
  • Keep the process clear.
  • Avoid unnecessary complexity.

The Reason

Why I Built OpenAgent

OpenAgent was created around a simple idea: real estate agents, developers and interior design studios should be able to access the same quality of digital marketing that big brands enjoy — without the agency layers and the complexity.

Advertising, SEO, websites and automation are often sold as separate services by separate vendors. I bring them together under one practical system, built specifically for how property and design clients actually choose who to work with.

The focus is not on making a business look bigger than it is. The focus is on doing useful work, communicating clearly and building marketing that makes sense for the business.
