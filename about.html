The approach

Useful marketing is built slowly, then kept sharp.

OpenAgent is deliberately narrow: two services, done properly. Performance marketing that earns its budget against an objective you chose, and a social media presence that publishes every week instead of disappearing in busy months.

Every project starts from your business reality: what you sell, who actually needs it, how they decide to buy and what your current marketing is already doing. The scope then stays practical and transparent — fixed deliverables, a working cadence and a price agreed before anything runs.

No fabricated proof, no inflated claims and no black-box handoff.

Capabilities

Two focused ways to help.

Performance Marketing

Google Ads and Meta Ads campaigns with strategy, management, tracking and reporting at the level your scope and package define. Creative and ad copy included where the scope says so.

Social Media Management

Monthly calendars, content creation for each platform, publishing, community management where the scope includes it, and a monthly numbers review.

Working style

What working with me looks like.

Direct communication with the person doing the work
A scope written around your current business reality — not a template upsell
Plain-language explanations; jargon only when you ask for it
Decisions grounded in real numbers, not opinions
One person accountable end to end
