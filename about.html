The approach

The work starts with how your business actually sells.

OpenAgent covers the whole path from first visit to enquiry, one written scope at a time. Human-reviewed content, a social media presence that publishes every week instead of disappearing in busy months, and paid ads that earn their budget against an objective you chose.

Every project starts from your business reality: what you sell, who actually needs it, how they decide to buy and what your current marketing is already doing. The scope then stays practical and transparent: fixed deliverables, a working cadence and a price agreed before anything runs.

The scope and the reports stay visible to you.
