Meta Ads vs Google Ads — Which for B2B Lead Generation?

By Abeer Nasir · OpenAgent

The question "Meta Ads or Google Ads?" comes up in almost every B2B lead-generation conversation. The short answer: they do two different jobs, and many growing businesses end up using both — just not on day one.

Meta Ads: demand creation

Meta (Facebook + Instagram) is where you show your offer to people who didn't know they needed it. It's a strong fit for visual products — perfume, beauty, apparel — because the creative does the selling. You reach cold audiences, build desire, and retarget the ones who engaged.

Best for: new brands, launch campaigns, retargeting, and anything visual. A small-budget Meta Ads test typically runs 7–14 days: lead with UGC-style video, keep the winner, and pause whoever underperforms.

Google Ads: demand capture

Google catches people who are already searching — "buy oud perfume" or "Shopify expert". They have intent; you just need to be there. Search ads typically convert well, but they only work if people already know the term they're searching.

Best for: proven products, high-intent keywords, and defending your brand name from competitors bidding on it.

Which first?

  • New offer, no search volume yet → start with Meta Ads to create demand.
  • People already searching for you → add Google Ads to capture them.
  • Small budget → pick one, get it working, then expand.

A simple rule

Use Meta to make people want it, Google to catch them when they go looking. Start with one channel, measure what matters (qualified leads and sales, not likes), and only add the second once the first is profitable.

How to decide in practice

It's rarely an either/or. Most accounts I plan end with both channels working different jobs: Meta for the top of the funnel, Google for the people already shopping. If you're starting from zero, pick the channel that matches where the demand lives today — if nobody is searching for you yet, run Meta first.
