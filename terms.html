1. Scope of Services

OpenAgent delivers digital marketing services including website development, Shopify development, SEO, ecommerce SEO, social media marketing, Meta, Google, TikTok and ChatGPT ads, lead generation, SaaS marketing and SaaS SEO. The specific deliverables, milestones and timelines are defined in your proposal or statement of work. Anything outside that scope is quoted separately before it starts.

2. Fees, Currency & Payment

Work is invoiced as agreed monthly retainers or project milestones. Fees are quoted in US dollars unless your proposal states another currency. Payment is due by the date on the invoice, and any bank, card or transfer fees and any taxes that apply in your country are paid by the client. Work may pause while an invoice is overdue.

Ad budgets paid to Meta, Google, TikTok or other platforms go directly from your payment method to those platforms and are separate from OpenAgent fees. Ad accounts, pixels, analytics and website accounts are set up in your name or business, so you keep them if we stop working together.
