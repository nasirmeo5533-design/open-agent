Prices shown on this website are starting prices. The final price depends on your business, the scope agreed on the free call, and the services you pick. The pricing calculator gives an estimate only — the confirmed amount is agreed before any work begins, so there are no surprises.

For Meta & Google Ads, the price is my management fee — setting up, testing, and optimizing your campaigns. The ad spend itself (the money paid to Facebook and Google to run the ads) is separate. You pay the platforms directly, so you always know what my fee is vs. what the platforms charge.

Payment terms are agreed on the free call and confirmed before work starts. Monthly services are billed monthly. One-time projects are billed per the agreed schedule. If payment is delayed, work may be paused until the account is settled.

Every package comes with a 3-day money-back guarantee. If you are not happy with the service within the first 3 days of the engagement, tell me and you don't pay. The guarantee does not cover ad spend already paid to Facebook or Google — that money belongs to the platforms and is refunded only according to their own policies.

Timelines are agreed per project on the free call. I commit to the deadlines we set, and I report progress in plain English. Some deliverables depend on you — for example, ad account access, product photos, or brand materials — and delays in receiving those may move the timeline.
