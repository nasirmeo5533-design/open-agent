Terms of Service

Last updated: November 28, 2026

These terms apply to every OpenAgent project. By approving a proposal and making the first payment, you agree to them.

1. Scope for Each Service

Every service has its own scope. The deliverables, timeline and price for each service you choose are written down and agreed before work begins.

2. How a Project Starts

First we talk about your business and what you need. The full project is then discussed in a meeting and confirmed by email. Work starts only after the scope is agreed.

All project communication happens over WhatsApp and email.

3. Payment

A 50% payment is due before the project starts. The remaining balance is due as set out in your proposal.

Payments are normally made directly to OpenAgent. If you have concerns about paying directly, the contract can be set up on Upwork instead, and payment then goes through Upwork.

4. Ad Spend

Ad spend is charged directly to your own ad account on your own card. It is paid to the platform (for example Meta or Google) and is separate from OpenAgent fees.

5. Multi-Service Discount

If you buy more than three services (four or more), you get a 10% discount on the total fee.

Each project includes two rounds of revisions, and no more. Any change after that is treated as new work and quoted separately.

No specific result is guaranteed. Rankings, leads and sales depend on many things outside anyone’s control. What you can count on is that the agreed work is done properly, with full effort.
