The Process

How I Work

No fluff. No handoffs. One person who owns your results from day one.

01

Understand Your Business

I learn about your business, your target clients, your past marketing, and your goals before recommending anything. No cookie-cutter strategies — everything starts with your specific situation.

02

Build the Plan

Based on what I learn, I map out the fastest path to results — which channels, which offer, which audience, and what timeline is realistic. You get a clear plan before any work begins.

03

Execute With Precision

I build your campaigns, automations, content systems, or Shopify setup with speed and attention to detail. You stay informed at every stage — no black boxes.

04

Go Live & Gather Data

Everything goes live and I track results from day one. Early data tells us what's working and what needs to be adjusted before we scale.

05

Improve Every Week

I review performance weekly and make data-driven improvements. The goal is compounding results — every week should be better than the last.
